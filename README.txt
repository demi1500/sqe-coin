SQE COIN - Android starter prototype

This is a local web prototype, not a deployed cryptocurrency or finished Android APK.

Quick test:
1. Open index.html in Chrome on Android or a desktop browser.
2. Choose Shqip, English, or Français.
3. Play the quick game, claim the daily demo reward, and answer the quiz.
4. Demo points are stored locally in that browser on that device.

Important:
- SQE points in this prototype have no monetary value.
- No wallet connection, blockchain transactions, user accounts, server, or withdrawals are included.
- Do not enter a wallet recovery phrase or private key into any app.
- A production Android app needs backend/security work, privacy terms, testing, and a separate Android build/signing process.
